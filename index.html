1. Introduction

Welcome to Fitness RPG.

Fitness RPG is a fitness application that combines exercise tracking, workout planning, experience points (XP), achievements and role-playing game elements.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, used, stored and shared when you use Fitness RPG.

2. Information Used by Fitness RPG

Fitness RPG uses information needed to provide its training and game features.

Depending on the features you use, this information may include:

  • Training preferences, goals and experience level.
  • Workout schedules, exercises, completed sessions and training history.
  • XP, character levels, achievements, missions and cosmetic rewards.
  • Body weight measurements entered for progress tracking.
  • Step-count information obtained from supported device sensors.
  • Application settings, language and selected visual theme.

This information is used to provide workout planning, progress tracking and game functionality.

3. Local Data Storage

Fitness RPG uses local device storage for application preferences and supported progress features.

Information stored locally may include workout settings, selected language, training progress and game preferences.

Locally stored data may be removed when you clear the application's storage or uninstall the application.

Some information processed by third-party services, such as advertising or payment providers, may be stored separately according to those providers' policies.

4. Physical Activity and Step Tracking

Fitness RPG offers optional step-tracking features on compatible Android devices.

When you enable step tracking, the application may request Android's physical activity permission.

Step information is used to display activity progress and support related in-app fitness features.

You can manage physical activity permissions through your device's Android settings.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

Fitness RPG is preparing to use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

When advertising is enabled, the application may display interstitial advertisements at natural transition points, such as after a completed workout.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from technical signals, IP addresses and advertising interactions, subject to applicable law and user privacy choices.

Advertising-related information may be used for delivering ads, measuring performance, preventing fraud and, where permitted, personalizing advertising.

6. Advertising Consent

Where required by applicable privacy laws, Fitness RPG uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy choices before requesting advertisements.

Available choices may include consenting, declining consent and managing advertising preferences.

The availability and presentation of these choices may vary based on applicable regulations and the user's location.

A user's decision not to consent does not prevent access to the core workout functionality of Fitness RPG.

7. In-App Purchases

Fitness RPG is preparing optional purchases for premium visual themes.

When these purchases become available, transactions on Android will be processed through Google Play Billing.

Google Play may process payment information, transaction identifiers and purchase details according to Google's own privacy policies.

Fitness RPG may use transaction information to verify purchases and determine access to purchased content.

Premium visual themes are optional and do not change the user's physical activity records.

8. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by third-party service providers when their features are enabled.

These providers may include:

  • Google AdMob for advertising and related measurement.
  • Google User Messaging Platform for managing privacy choices.
  • Google Play for processing in-app purchases and related transactions.

The information processed by each provider depends on the services enabled in the application.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Fitness RPG stores certain application information locally while the application is installed and its storage is retained.

You can remove local application data through Android's application settings.

You can also contact us to ask questions about data handling or request assistance with a privacy-related matter.

Data processed independently by Google or other service providers is subject to the relevant provider's retention and deletion practices.
